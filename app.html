1. Terms of Use

Governs every member's use of the register.

Eligibility

You must be at least 18, able to form a binding contract under Indian law, and a genuine, identifiable working professional. Applications that misrepresent identity, employer, qualification, or domain may be rejected, and membership may be revoked without notice if discovered later.

Nature of the service

MahaYukti is a private register that facilitates introductions between members. It is not a staffing agency, broker, dealer, financial advisor, law firm, or a party to any engagement, referral, sale, or investment that results from a connection made here.

Acceptable use

No impersonating another member, firm, or institution; no posting client-identifying information without that client's consent; no unsolicited bulk messaging or harvesting Directory contact details for mass outreach.

Membership tiers

Member tier is free and instant on a complete application — no review queue. Participant and higher tiers are granted automatically on objective, disclosed criteria (Account → Membership tier) and can never be purchased, gifted, or applied for directly.

Suspension & termination

We may suspend or terminate an account for breach of these Terms or the Community Guidelines, or on reasonable suspicion of fraud, with written reasons provided on request.

Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by Indian law, our aggregate liability to any member is limited to the fees (if any) paid by that member in the preceding 12 months. We are not liable for indirect, consequential, or reputational loss arising from interactions between members.

Governing law

These Terms are governed by the laws of India. Courts at Bengaluru, Karnataka have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute, subject to the grievance process in Section 4 being followed first.

2. Privacy Policy

Aligned to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

What we collect

Identity and contact details you provide on application; professional details (domain, employer, credentials); content you post; device/usage data; and, where you choose to connect it, basic profile data from LinkedIn. We never display your phone number or email to another member — that's a platform-level rule, not a setting.

Why we process it

To verify eligibility; operate the Directory, Feed, Messages, and The Exchange; prevent fraud and abuse; respond to legal process; and — only with your separate, specific consent — show you relevant ads.

Your rights as a Data Principal

Access a copy of your data (Account → Download my data) · correct or update it at any time · request erasure, subject to records we must legally retain (e.g. fraud or dispute records) · withdraw consent for optional processing (e.g. ads) without affecting core membership · nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.

Retention & cross-border transfer

Personal data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes above, or as required by law, and is not transferred outside India except to jurisdictions not restricted by the Central Government under the DPDP Act, and only under contractual safeguards. We use Supabase for database/auth hosting and standard cloud infrastructure, bound by contract to protect your data; we do not sell it.

Data Protection queries

info@mahayukti.com, routed to our Grievance Officer (Section 4).

3. Community Guidelines

MahaYukti works because the register is real — real people, real credentials, real organisations.

Expected conduct

Represent yourself, your firm, and your credentials accurately at all times. Treat every member's and every client's confidentiality as the default — share only what you have the right to share. Keep Exchange briefs and Marketplace listings genuine; no shell listings, no undisclosed self-dealing.

Prohibited content

Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, sexually explicit content, content that infringes IP or confidentiality obligations, unverifiable financial claims, and anything unlawful under Indian law (including the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita and the IT Act, 2000) will be removed and may result in suspension.

Enforcement

Reports are reviewed by a real admin queue, not an automated filter. First breach: warning + content removal. Repeat or severe breach: tier downgrade or termination.

4. Grievance Redressal Mechanism

Published in compliance with Rule 3(2) of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.

Grievance Officer: Vedant Rungta
Email: info@mahayukti.com · Jurisdiction: Bengaluru, Karnataka · Response: acknowledged within 24 hours, resolved within 15 days (72 hours for content-removal requests concerning impersonation or non-consensual imagery).
Escalation

If unresolved, a member may escalate to the Grievance Appellate Committee constituted under the IT Rules, or to the appropriate consumer forum or court.

5. Exchange & Marketplace Disclaimer

MahaYukti is an intermediary only. We do not inspect, warrant, broker, underwrite, insure, or take any fee or commission from goods, services, capital, co-founder arrangements, or any other transaction arranged between members via The Exchange or Marketplace. Members transact entirely at their own risk and are solely responsible for their own diligence, their own agreements, and resolving any dispute between themselves.

Seller Packages (Marketplace)

The Seller Package fee is a flat, fixed-term listing fee for the right to post on the Marketplace — it is not a commission, success fee, or cut of the eventual sale price, and is never described as one in our marketing.

6. Refund & Cancellation Policy

Seller Packages: non-refundable once activated by an admin. A pro-rated refund is available only if requested in writing within 24 hours of activation and before any listing has been published under it.

Event hosting (where a fee applies): fully refundable if cancelled 7+ days before the event; 50% within 7 days; non-refundable within 48 hours, except where MahaYukti cancels the event.

Advertising: governed by the separate Advertiser Agreement issued at the time of booking.

7. "Verified Member" Disclaimer

A "Verified" badge confirms that MahaYukti checked the specific credential(s) stated on that member's profile (e.g. a professional registration number, employer domain email, or institutional affiliation) at the time of verification. It is not a certification of a member's current standing, solvency, competence, or fitness for any particular engagement, and we disclaim all liability arising from reliance on the badge.

8. Intermediary & Safe Harbor Notice

MahaYukti is an "intermediary" as defined under Section 2(1)(w) of the Information Technology Act, 2000, and claims safe-harbor protection under Section 79 for content posted by members. Views expressed in posts, briefs, listings, and Communities are those of the posting member alone and are not endorsed by MahaYukti. Unlawful or infringing content, or content in breach of these policies, will be taken down on notice per Section 4 above.

9. Cookie Policy

We use strictly-necessary cookies to keep you signed in and secure the session. With your separate consent, we use analytics cookies to understand feature usage and, only if you opt in, advertising cookies for the Advertise on MahaYukti program. Withdraw non-essential consent at any time from Account → Settings & Privacy.

Questions about any policy above, or about your data: info@mahayukti.com.

FAQs & Support

This is for help using MahaYukti itself — a bug, a question about how something works, your account. For professional questions you want other members to weigh in on, that's MY Help instead.

Frequently asked
How do I become a Member, and what about higher tiers?
Member tier approves instantly when you apply — no waiting on a reviewer. Participant and above are earned automatically through real activity on the register later; nobody applies for them directly or pays their way in.
Who can message me?
Set this yourself under Settings & Privacy — "Everyone" or "Connections only." Blocking someone always overrides it in both directions, whatever the setting.
How do I report a post or a member?
Every post has a Report link. For a member specifically, open their profile and use Mute (hides their posts from your feed) or Block (also stops messages and connections both ways).
Can I pause or delete my account?
Both live under Settings & Privacy. Deactivating is immediate and reversible by an admin; permanent deletion is reviewed by an admin too — it's never instant self-service, by design.
What's the difference between MY Help and this page?
MY Help is members helping members — post a professional question, get replies from peers. This page is for the app itself not working as expected, or not understanding how a feature works.
