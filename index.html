Entry 02 — How the Registry Is Organised

Two axes. One register.

Bucket & Vertical

A bucket is a macro domain community — Legal, CA & Finance, Technology & Cybersecurity, and others still coming. A vertical is the specific tag within it — inside CA & Finance, that might read CA, VC, private lender, or banker. Every member sits in a bucket, tagged by vertical.

Tier

Tier is a separate axis entirely — it tracks standing, not profession. It runs Member → Participant → Bucket Head → Council, and is earned through contribution, never purchased. See the full tier table under Network.

Register — Network Structure

Open to all.
Selective by design.

Every application is reviewed individually. Admission is by scrutiny — credentials, domain expertise, and professional intent are all considered.

Tier — Standing, Not Profession

Member → Participant →
Bucket Head → Council.

TierHow it's reachedWhat it holds
MemberOpen application, verified credentialFree, forever. No platform cut. No fees between members. Directory listing, MY Help access, lead routing.
ParticipantActive, proven contribution as a MemberActive service listing, domain forums, priority referral routing, MY Help mentoring.
Bucket HeadOne per bucket — direct-invited industry leader, or promoted from within for real contributionOwns vetting of new applicants in that bucket. Invite-only, annual fee.
CouncilThe sitting Bucket Heads, collectively, plus the founderNetwork governance, standards, and dispute resolution. Invite-only, annual fee.

Member tier is free and stays free — no registration fee, no annual fee, no charges between members, ever. The Bucket Head / Council tier is invite-only and carries an annual fee; this is the network's actual monetisation layer, separate entirely from how Members transact with each other.

Register — Service Lines

Four service lines.
One ecosystem.

Every need, every scale — from structured advisory to discreet personal facilitation.

01 — MY Advisory

MY Advisory

Research, analysis, strategic intelligence, and counsel — for corporates, SMEs, political clients, and institutions working through complex mandates.

Example: a corporate client planning an acquisition needs background intelligence. A legal expert, a forensic CA, a market researcher, and an OSINT specialist coordinate through MY Advisory to deliver one complete intelligence brief — not four separate invoices.

02 — MY Consultancy

MY Consultancy

Expert-to-client professional services across every domain — the right expert matched to the right client, from first-generation entrepreneurs to listed corporations.

Example: a first-generation entrepreneur needs a bank-loan DPR, legal structuring, and a feasibility study. MY Consultancy coordinates a CA, a lawyer, and a market researcher around one client.

03 — MY Concierge

MY Concierge

Bespoke facilitation for individuals and organisations navigating complex, sensitive, or time-critical circumstances — discreet, and available on short notice.

MY Concierge is not a public service. It is accessible to members and referred clients only, and the first conversation is always confidential.

Enquire Privately
04 — MY Help

MY Help

The community layer — peer connection, mentoring, referrals, knowledge exchange, and buyer–seller introductions. No transaction fees. No platform cuts.

Example: a law graduate in Patna is paired with a senior corporate advocate in Delhi — building in months what otherwise takes years.
