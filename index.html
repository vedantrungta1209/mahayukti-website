What MahaYukti is

Not a job board. Not a platform. A register.

Members join a private, admission-gated register — not a marketplace. There's no platform cut on what members earn from each other, and nobody buys or applies their way into a senior tier.

Once you're in

A real network, not a directory

Membership isn't just a listing. Every member gets a private feed, direct messages, and the tools below — built for professionals who want to actually use the register, not just appear in it.

A feed, scoped to your sector
Share updates with your own vertical, your connections, or everyone — your choice each time. Government and market updates reach every member regardless.
Communities
Open topic discussions, and institution-verified alumni groups — the same mechanism, a verification check for the ones that need one.
The Exchange
Post a real need — a service request, a co-founder search, a business for sale, capital you're raising or deploying — directly to the register.
Marketplace
Buy and sell equipment, vehicles, and supplies between verified members only — never indexed outside MahaYukti.
Events
Summits, roundtables, and alumni gatherings hosted by members and organisations — some open, some invite-only.
Connect and Follow, separately
Connect when you actually know someone — it needs their acceptance. Follow just to see someone's posts — it doesn't.
